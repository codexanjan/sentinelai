Sentinel’s additions focus on a self-recorded recovery drill, one-account reveal and optional fresh-code copying. The reviewed feature lists do not establish exhaustive market absence. No claim of universal uniqueness or superiority is made. No competitor trademarks or interface artwork are copied.
